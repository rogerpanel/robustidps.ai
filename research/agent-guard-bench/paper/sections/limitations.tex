\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\emph{Replay is not a live agent.} Ground-truth calls are one path through each task; a model may take others, retry after a refusal, or split an attack differently. Replay measures the filter on fixed call sequences and treats approved requests as approved; it does not measure how often people approve attacks, which a user study would. \emph{Coverage.} Nine AgentDojo injection tasks have no ground-truth calls and are excluded; with \AdGoals{} goals, intervals are wide, and a zero count bounds the goal-level rate only at \AdRuleOfThree{}. \emph{Policies.} The generated packs are simple; hand-written packs would do better on burden, but would invite tuning to the benchmark, which we avoided. The conservative flow rule is ours, though it is mechanical and was not tuned per task. \emph{Internal suites} were written alongside the packs by the authors and confirm behaviour rather than estimate accuracy. \emph{Deferral} is analysed through eligibility and a covering threshold, the worst case for a low-denial history, rather than through months of real decisions. \emph{Hosts} were verified at specific versions; hook contracts change, and the self-test should be rerun after upgrades. \emph{Adaptive attacks} against the policy itself (searching for allowed call sequences that achieve a goal) were not run.
