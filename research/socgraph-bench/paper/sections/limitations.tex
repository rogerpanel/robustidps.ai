\section{Scope and Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Benchmark scale and ground truth} \bench has 21 lab-recorded tasks, one technique per recording, so
results do not establish performance on enterprise-scale, multi-host campaigns; the graph builder and selectors
apply unchanged to further recordings and corpora~\cite{darpa_tc,landauer2023maintainable,grimmer2023lidds}.
Ground truth follows a stated rule applied through published selectors; other analysts could draw the core set
differently~\cite{jiang2025orthrus,bilot2025simpler}. Both answer versions and the rescoring tool are released,
so the rule can be changed and every run re-evaluated, as we did for v1.1.

\paragraph{Models and variance} Each condition has three repeats; paired task-level intervals resolve reward
differences of about $\pm0.02$, and the task-clustered permutation tests show which effects rest on too few
tasks to be conclusive (\S\ref{sec:models}). The ablation uses one model at one effort level, and with GPT-6
astra the hard budget did act, so the null effect of the safeguards is model-specific. Both models are closed
and may change; every call is traced.

\paragraph{Contamination and adversary strength} The recordings are public and may have been seen in
training~\cite{sainz2023contamination}; the tasks re-express them as graphs with opaque identifiers and new
alerts, and the \emph{blind} variant removes their label cues without changing results. Mimicry renames strings
only, so its effect is a lower bound: structural mimicry and instructions planted in
telemetry~\cite{greshake2023indirect,debenedetti2024agentdojo} are stronger and are the next step. Some renamed
tasks are genuinely ambiguous from provenance alone; RQ7 measures this directly, finding that neither
canonicalisation nor a prompt rule restores the renamed attacks -- they are under-determined by the graph, not
merely mislabelled.

\paragraph{Protocol} The prompt and safeguards were fixed before the study runs, and results are broken down
per class and platform~\cite{arp2022dosdonts}. All results are on \bench with our scorer, not on the official
competition tasks.
