# Manuscript (Overleaf-ready)

`main.tex` compiles with **pdfLaTeX + BibTeX**, the Overleaf default.

## Open in Overleaf

1. Zip this `paper/` folder (or use the `socgraph-paper-overleaf.zip` in the release).
2. Overleaf: **New Project > Upload Project**, choose the zip.
3. Menu > Compiler: pdfLaTeX; Main document: `main.tex`. Recompile.

Locally: `latexmk -pdf main.tex`.

## Before submitting

- Replace the repository URL placeholder in the `\thanks{}` of `main.tex`
  (`https://github.com/<user>/socgraph-bench`) and check the affiliation.
- Add the study results (below); the sections that depend on them switch on
  automatically (`\ResStudyAvailable`).
- Write the interpretation of the ablation in `sections/results.tex` (marked
  with a comment) once the numbers are in.
- Bibliography: every entry in `references.bib` was checked against an
  authoritative source; `references_verification.md` lists the source per
  entry and the fields left out where they could not be confirmed (mostly
  DOIs and some page ranges). If the venue's style requires DOIs, copy them
  from the publisher pages listed there. Three page ranges are flagged for a
  check on IEEE Xplore (HOLMES, RapSheet, FLASH).

## Other templates

Two builds share `preamble.tex` and `content.tex`:

- `main.tex`: IEEEtran journal mode with author block (IEEE TIFS / TDSC).
- `main-conference.tex`: IEEEtran `conference,compsoc`, anonymous, for
  double-blind review at IEEE SaTML or IEEE S&P. The platform name is replaced
  by a neutral phrase (`\ReviewUI`), and the repository link is omitted. Its
  main text ends on page 13 and the whole PDF is 18 pages, as the IEEE S&P
  2027 CFP requires. In this build the pilot, the engineering observations
  and the ethics and generative-AI sections move to the appendix, and the
  full method prompt, the per-task matrix, the breakdown table and the type
  table are left to the artifact (`\ifconf` in the sources). In Overleaf,
  set *Main document* to `main-conference.tex` to build it. See
  `SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md` before submitting.

For an Elsevier journal (e.g. Computers & Security), switch to `elsarticle`:
replace the class and the `\author`/`\thanks` block with elsarticle's
`\author`/`\affiliation`, move the abstract and keywords into the
`frontmatter`, and use `\bibliographystyle{elsarticle-num}`. Figures and
tables are standard LaTeX and carry over.

## Structure

`sections/`: abstract, introduction, background (related work), problem
(preliminaries and problem statement), method, benchmark, experiments,
results, discussion, limitations (with ethics and availability), conclusion,
appendix (prompts, reproduction). `figures/`: TikZ/pgfplots sources.
